# hpr-nye-countdown
The Annual 26 Hour New Year's Eve Show countdown to 12-31 10:00 UTC

Welcome every TimeZone!

If you keep talking, we'll keep recording.

For those who don't know, on New Year's Eve 2026-12-31 10:00 UTC (5:00 AM EST), we will have a recording going on the HPR Mumble server for anyone to come on and say "Happy New Year" and talk about whatever they want. We will leave the recording going until at least 2027-01-01 12:00 UTC (7:00 AM EST), and keep recording until the conversation stops.

So please stop in. Say "Hi" and maybe join in the conversation with other HPR listeners and contributors. It's always a good time!!
